Ajayeb is an unofficial, non-commercial fan implementation of the rules of 7 Wonders Duel by Antoine Bauza and Bruno Cathala (Repos Production). It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Repos Production or Asmodee; 7 Wonders Duel is their trademark.

If you enjoy Ajayeb, please buy the real game: <https://www.rprod.com/en/games/7-wonders-duel>.

Ajayeb shows no ads and makes no money. We comply promptly with any takedown request from the rights holders; please open an issue at <https://github.com/NightMachinery/ajayeb/issues>.

## What is original here

All code, visual art, icons and rules text in this repository are original work. The third-party CC0 sounds are credited separately in the license record. We do not use the official card, wonder or box art, the logo, the official iconography, the card-frame layout or the rulebook text. Game mechanics and numeric card facts are re-expressed in our own words and data, with their provenance recorded.
